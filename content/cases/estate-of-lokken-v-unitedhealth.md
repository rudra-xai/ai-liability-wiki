*Estate of Gene B. Lokken v. UnitedHealth Group, Inc.* is a proposed class action by Medicare Advantage members and their estates. It alleges that UnitedHealth and its subsidiary naviHealth used the [[nh-predict|nH Predict]] algorithm to cut off coverage for post-acute care, such as skilled-nursing stays, in place of individual clinical judgment.[^complaint]

## Claims

Plaintiffs allege breach of contract, breach of the implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing, unjust enrichment, insurance bad faith and state consumer-protection violations. They allege the algorithm's predictions were applied rigidly and that most appealed denials were reversed. UnitedHealth disputes the allegations and says the tool guides rather than makes coverage decisions.[^complaint]

## 2025 ruling

In February 2025 the court dismissed most claims as preempted by the Medicare Act but allowed the breach-of-contract and implied-covenant claims to proceed, because they turn on the insurer's own policy promises about how coverage decisions would be made.[^mtd]

## Why it matters for insurers

The case is a template for challenges to **insurers' own use of AI** in claims handling, and it shows why regulators expect documented human oversight of algorithmic decisions. See [[ai-in-insurance-underwriting-and-claims]] and [[naic-model-bulletin]].
