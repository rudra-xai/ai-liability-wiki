*Amazon v. Perplexity* is an early test of whether an AI agent acting for a user may access a third-party website against the site owner's wishes. Amazon alleges that Perplexity's Comet browser agent logged into customers' Amazon accounts and made purchases while disguising its automated nature, in breach of computer-access laws and Amazon's conditions of use.

## October 2026 motion

In an October 13, 2026 motion, Perplexity asked the court to dismiss Amazon's amended claims. It disputes Amazon's theory that it wrongfully induced customers to breach their agreements with Amazon.[^motion][^tfl]

## Why it matters for insurers

Agentic commerce spreads liability across three parties: the user, the agent provider and the website. How this case allocates responsibility will shape technology E&O and cyber exposure for agent developers. See [[ai-agents-and-liability]].
