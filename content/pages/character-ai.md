**Character.AI** is a consumer app, operated by Character Technologies, Inc., that lets users converse with AI "characters". It has faced several lawsuits alleging harm to minors, most prominently [[garcia-v-character-technologies]], which settled in 2026 after a 2025 ruling that treated the app as a product for design-defect claims. See [[chatbot-product-liability]].
