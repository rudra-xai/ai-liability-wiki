The **National Institute of Standards and Technology** (NIST) is a U.S. Department of Commerce agency that develops measurement standards and voluntary frameworks. Its [[nist-ai-rmf|AI Risk Management Framework]] and generative-AI profile are among the most cited AI governance references in U.S. law and insurance underwriting.
