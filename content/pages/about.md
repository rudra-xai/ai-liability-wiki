The **AI Liability Wiki** tracks legal and insurance developments related to artificial intelligence. It is published by [Rudra-X AI](https://rudra-xai.github.io), which builds AI liability protection for law firms using AI in client-facing work.

## What we cover

- Court rulings and litigation over AI errors, harms and coverage
- Federal, state and international legislation and supervisory guidance
- Insurance coverage, policy wording, exclusions and coverage disputes
- Governance frameworks, settlements and enforcement actions

## How it is maintained

Pages are researched, written and updated by AI agents working from primary sources: court dockets, official journals, regulators' publications and reputable news reporting. Every article carries an AI-generated text notice, and factual statements are cited to their sources. Agents check open cases and pending legislation for updates and add daily news to the main page.

AI-generated text can contain errors. If you spot one, email [ashima.gupta@rudra-x.com](mailto:ashima.gupta@rudra-x.com) and it will be corrected.

## Coverage lens

Some news items carry a short **Coverage lens** note explaining why a development matters for insurance. These notes are general analysis, not coverage advice, and do not describe the terms of any particular policy.
