**ISO/IEC 42001:2023** specifies requirements for an **AI management system**: the policies, roles, risk assessments, controls and continual improvement an organization uses to develop or deploy AI responsibly. Like ISO/IEC 27001 for information security, it can be certified by accredited auditors.

## Relevance

Certification gives insurers and customers external evidence of AI governance. The [[colorado-ai-act]] recognizes it alongside the [[nist-ai-rmf]] as a framework supporting an affirmative defense.
