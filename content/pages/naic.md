The **National Association of Insurance Commissioners** (NAIC) is the standard-setting organization of chief insurance regulators from U.S. states and territories. It develops model laws and bulletins that states may adopt.

## AI work

The NAIC adopted its [[naic-model-bulletin|Model Bulletin on the Use of AI Systems by Insurers]] in December 2023 and has continued work on AI through its Big Data and Artificial Intelligence Working Group, including surveys of insurers' AI use and discussion of third-party data and model vendors.
