## What we collect

- **Preferences in your browser:** your theme choice and the time of your last visit (used to mark new stories) are stored in your own browser's local storage. They are never sent to us.

The site uses no advertising trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and video thumbnails from YouTube, which may log your IP address under their own policies.
