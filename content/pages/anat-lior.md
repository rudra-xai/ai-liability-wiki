**Anat Lior** is a legal scholar whose research addresses tort liability for AI and the role of insurance in managing AI risk.

## Selected work

- "Insuring AI: The Role of Insurance in Artificial Intelligence Regulation", *Harvard Journal of Law & Technology* (2022), which argues that insurers can act as de facto regulators of AI through underwriting and pricing.
- Scholarship on applying existing liability doctrines, such as vicarious liability, to harms caused by AI systems.
