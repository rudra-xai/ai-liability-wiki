The **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** (AI RMF 1.0) is a voluntary framework for managing risks across the AI life cycle. It is organized around four functions: **Govern**, **Map**, **Measure** and **Manage**.[^rmf]

## Generative AI profile

NIST AI 600-1, published in July 2024, applies the framework to generative AI, listing risks such as confabulation (hallucination), data privacy, information integrity and intellectual property, with suggested actions for each.

## Legal and insurance relevance

Several state laws, including the [[colorado-ai-act]], treat conformity with the AI RMF as supporting an affirmative defense, and insurers increasingly ask about it during underwriting.

[^rmf]: [NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) (Jan. 26, 2023).
