**Ryan Abbott** is a law professor and physician known for work on how law should treat AI-generated activity.

## Selected work

- *The Reasonable Robot: Artificial Intelligence and the Law* (Cambridge University Press, 2020), which argues for **AI legal neutrality**: the law should not discriminate between AI and human behavior, including in tort, where AI conduct could be judged by a negligence standard.
- Led the Artificial Inventor Project, test cases seeking patents naming the DABUS AI system as inventor.
