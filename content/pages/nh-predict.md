**nH Predict** is a predictive tool developed by naviHealth, a UnitedHealth Group subsidiary, that estimates patients' expected post-acute-care needs, such as length of stay in a skilled-nursing facility.

## Legal significance

The tool is at the center of [[estate-of-lokken-v-unitedhealth]], which alleges it was used to cut off Medicare Advantage coverage in place of individual clinical judgment. UnitedHealth says the tool informs, rather than makes, coverage decisions. The case has become a reference point for regulators' expectations of human review in [[ai-in-insurance-underwriting-and-claims|insurers' use of AI]].
