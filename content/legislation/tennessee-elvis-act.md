The **ELVIS Act** amended Tennessee's Personal Rights Protection Act to add **voice**, including simulated voice, to the protected attributes of name, image and likeness. It creates civil liability for publishing or distributing an individual's voice or likeness without authorization, and for distributing technology whose primary purpose is producing such unauthorized imitations.[^act]

## Why it matters

The Act was the first U.S. state law aimed specifically at AI voice cloning. It expands media-liability and IP-infringement exposure for studios, platforms, advertisers and political campaigns using generative audio and video.

[^act]: Tenn. Pub. Ch. 588 (2024), amending Tenn. Code Ann. § 47-25-1101 et seq.
