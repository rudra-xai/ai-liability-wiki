The **Colorado Artificial Intelligence Act** was the first comprehensive U.S. state law on high-risk AI. It requires developers and deployers of "high-risk artificial intelligence systems", those that make or substantially factor into consequential decisions, to use reasonable care to protect consumers from algorithmic discrimination.[^bill]

## Main duties

- **Developers** must give deployers documentation on intended uses, known risks and data, and publish summaries of their high-risk systems.
- **Deployers** must run a risk-management program, complete impact assessments, notify consumers when AI is used in a consequential decision and offer an explanation and appeal for adverse decisions.
- Compliance with recognized frameworks such as the [[nist-ai-rmf|NIST AI RMF]] or [[iso-iec-42001|ISO/IEC 42001]] supports an affirmative defense.[^bill]

## Insurance

Insurance is listed as a consequential-decision area. Colorado insurers are also subject to the earlier SB 21-169, which bars unfair discrimination through external consumer data and algorithms and is implemented by Division of Insurance regulations.

[^bill]: [Colorado SB 24-205, Consumer Protections for Artificial Intelligence](https://leg.colorado.gov/bills/sb24-205).
