The **Texas Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act** (TRAIGA) takes a narrower approach than Colorado's. Rather than broad duties for high-risk systems, it prohibits developing or deploying AI with intent to cause specified harms, such as inciting self-harm or unlawful discrimination, and requires government agencies to disclose AI use to consumers.[^hb149]

## Features

- Enforcement by the Attorney General, with notice and an opportunity to cure before penalties.
- A **regulatory sandbox** letting approved companies test AI products under relaxed state rules.
- A Texas Artificial Intelligence Council to advise on AI policy.[^hb149]
