**Insurance Circular Letter No. 7 (2024)** sets out the New York Department of Financial Services' expectations for insurers that use artificial intelligence systems or external consumer data and information sources (ECDIS) in underwriting and pricing.[^cl7]

## Expectations

- Insurers must be able to show that ECDIS and AI systems **do not use or proxy for protected classes** and do not produce unfair or unlawful discrimination, including through quantitative testing.
- Boards and senior management are responsible for **governance and risk management** of AI, including vendor oversight.
- Consumers must receive specific reasons for adverse underwriting or pricing decisions, including when AI was involved.[^cl7]
